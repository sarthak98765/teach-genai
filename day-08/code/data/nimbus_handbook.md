# 1. Welcome to Nimbus Labs

Nimbus Labs Private Limited is a software company founded in 2021 and headquartered in Bengaluru, India. We build Nimbus Ledger, a cloud accounting product used by more than 4,000 small businesses, and Nimbus Pay, a payments layer that plugs into it. As of 1 January 2026 we are 182 people across two offices and remote locations.

This handbook explains how we work: the policies everyone follows, what the company offers you as an employee, and how money moves inside the company. It applies to every full-time employee of Nimbus Labs in India. Interns and contractors follow their own agreements, and where those agreements are silent, this handbook applies.

## Our mission and values

Our mission is to make accounting invisible for small businesses. We hold ourselves to four values:

- **Customers first.** Every decision starts with what is best for the business owner using our product.
- **Write it down.** Decisions, plans and policies are written, so that people who were not in the room can follow them.
- **Own the outcome.** If you start something, you see it through or hand it over clearly.
- **Be kind and direct.** Disagree openly, give feedback early, and assume good intent.

## 2. Code of Conduct

This code applies to every employee, at the office, while working remotely, at client sites and at company events. It also applies online: on Slack, email, social media and in our product's customer community.

### Expected behaviour

- Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect, whatever their role, background or seniority.
- Follow the law in every country we operate in, including tax, data protection and labour laws.
- Be honest in all records you create: timesheets, expense claims, sales figures, test results and reports.
- Raise concerns early. Silence about a problem you have seen is itself a breach of this code.
- Use company resources (money, laptops, accounts, time) for company work.

### Behaviour we do not accept

- Harassment, bullying, discrimination or threats of any kind.
- Working under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Alcohol at approved company events is allowed in moderation.
- Sharing confidential information without permission, including customer data, salaries and unreleased plans.
- Accepting bribes, or offering anything of value to win business improperly.
- Retaliating against anyone who reported a concern in good faith.

### Social media

You may say you work at Nimbus Labs on your personal profiles. Do not speak on behalf of the company unless the Marketing team has asked you to. Never post screenshots of internal tools, customer names or dashboards. If a journalist contacts you, forward them to press@nimbuslabs.example and do not comment.

## Consequences

Breaches of this code are handled under the disciplinary process in the Separation and Exit chapter. Depending on how serious it is, the outcome can be a verbal warning, a written warning, a final written warning, or termination. Fraud, violence, sexual harassment and theft of customer data can lead to immediate termination without notice.

## Reporting a concern

Report concerns to your manager, to the People team, or anonymously through the Ethics Line at ethics.nimbuslabs.example. Reports to the Ethics Line go to the Chief People Officer and an independent board member. Every report gets a written acknowledgement within 3 working days.

# 3. Working Hours, Attendance and Hybrid Work

## Core hours

The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST: be reachable during this window unless you are on leave or have blocked focus time on your calendar. Outside core hours, plan your day with your team.

Customer Support and Site Reliability Engineering run on rosters that cover weekends. People on these rosters get a compensatory off for every weekend day worked, to be used within 60 days.

## Hybrid work

Nimbus Labs is a hybrid company. Employees attached to an office work from the office on **Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday** and may work from home on Monday and Friday. Teams may choose to swap one office day with written approval from their department head.

Employees who work from home receive a **work-from-home stipend of Rs. 2,000 per month**, paid with salary, to cover internet and electricity. Fully remote employees receive the same stipend. (See the Addendum for the revised amount.)

## Attendance

Office attendance is recorded by the access card at the main door. There is no punching in or out for working hours. If you will miss an office day, tell your manager on Slack before 10:00 that day.

## Overtime

We do not expect regular late nights. If your team regularly works beyond 45 hours a week, your manager must raise it with the department head. Employees covered by the Shops and Establishments Act overtime rules are paid overtime at twice the hourly rate, as the law requires.

## Working from another city

You may work from another city in India for up to 20 working days a year, with your manager's approval, as long as you keep core hours. Working from outside India

needs approval from both HR and Finance, because of tax and data rules, and is limited to 15 working days a year.

## 4. Information Security and Acceptable Use

We hold the financial records of thousands of businesses. A single leak could end the company. Every employee is responsible for security.

### Accounts and passwords

- Use the company password manager (1Password) for every work account.
- Turn on multi-factor authentication on every account that supports it. Hardware keys are issued to engineers with production access.
- Never share your password or MFA codes, not even with IT. IT will never ask for them.
- Lock your screen whenever you step away (Ctrl+Cmd+Q on a Mac).

### Data classification

|  Class | Examples | Rules  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Public | Website, blog posts, published docs | Can be shared freely  |
|  Internal | Roadmaps, this handbook, Slack | Employees only  |
|  Confidential | Salaries, contracts, board decks | Named people only, never on personal devices  |
|  Restricted | Customer financial data, API keys | Production systems only, access logged and reviewed  |

### Using AI tools

You may use the AI assistants approved by IT (listed on the IT wiki) for work. Never paste Confidential or Restricted data, including customer records, source code from private repositories or API keys, into any AI tool that is not on the approved list.

### Devices

Company data may only be stored on company laptops, which are encrypted and managed by IT. You may read email and Slack on a personal phone only after installing the device management profile. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to it-security@nimbuslabs.example within 1 hour.

### Security incidents

If you click a suspicious link, see data where it should not be, or think an account was taken over, report it in #security-incident immediately. Reporting quickly is never punished. Hiding an incident is a disciplinary matter.

## Security training

All employees complete security training within 14 days of joining and again every year in January. Phishing tests run every quarter.

## 5. Customer Data and Privacy

Our customers trust us with their invoices, bank statements, payroll and tax filings. Handling that data well is part of every job here, not only the security team's. This chapter follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and our customer contracts.

### Principles

- **Purpose:** use customer data only to provide and improve the service the customer signed up for.
- **Minimum access:** ask for access only to the data you need, for as long as you need it.
- **No copies:** never download customer data to your laptop, a spreadsheet or a personal drive.
- **Anonymise for testing:** test and staging environments use generated or masked data only.

### Access to production data

Access to production databases is granted through a ticket on Pulse, approved by the data owner, and expires after **8 hours**. Every query on production is logged. The Security team reviews access logs every week, and any access without a linked ticket is investigated.

### Customer requests

Customers and their employees may ask to see, correct or delete their personal data. Forward any such request to privacy@nimbuslabs.example within 1 working day. The Privacy team responds within 30 days.

### Data retention

|  Data | Kept for | Why  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Accounting records of active customers | While the account is active | To run the service  |
|  Accounting records after a customer leaves | 8 years | Indian tax and company law  |
|  Support chats and emails | 3 years | Quality and disputes  |

|  Application logs | 180 days | Security investigations  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Job applicant data (not hired) | 12 months | Future openings, with consent  |

## Personal data breaches

A suspected breach of personal data is a security incident: report it in #security-incident straight away. The Privacy team decides, with Legal, whether the Data Protection Board and the affected customers must be told, and does so without delay.

## Sharing data with vendors

Customer data may be shared with a vendor only if the vendor has passed the IT security review and signed a data processing agreement. The current list of approved sub-processors is published on our website.

## 7. Anti-Harassment (POSH) and Grievances

Nimbus Labs follows the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (the POSH Act). Our policy protects employees of all genders, as well as interns, contractors and visitors.

### What counts as sexual harassment

Any unwelcome sexual behaviour, directly or by implication: physical contact or advances, a demand or request for sexual favours, sexually coloured remarks, showing pornography, or any other unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature. It applies at the office, at events, on business travel, and on chat and video calls.

### Internal Committee

Our Internal Committee (IC) has four members. It is chaired by a senior woman employee and includes an external member from an NGO. The current members are listed on the People wiki. You can reach the committee at ic@nimbuslabs.example.

### How a complaint works

- File a written complaint with the IC within 3 months of the incident (the IC can extend this by another 3 months for good reasons).
- The IC may try conciliation if the person complaining asks for it. No money settlement can be part of conciliation.
- Otherwise the IC runs an inquiry and finishes it within 90 days.
- The IC sends its report and recommendations to management within 10 days of finishing the inquiry, and management acts on them within 60 days.
- Everything about the complaint is confidential.

### Other grievances

For any other workplace problem (with a manager, a team, a policy or pay), talk to your manager first. If that does not resolve it within 10 working days, raise it with your HR Business Partner, and then with the Chief People Officer. You can skip any step if the problem involves that person.

You may work on side projects and open source outside working hours, on your own devices, as long as they do not compete with Nimbus Labs and do not use company data or code. Paid work for another company needs written approval from your department head.

## Gifts and hospitality

You may accept a gift from a vendor or customer worth up to Rs. 2,500. Anything more must be declared on Pulse and is usually donated or shared with the team. Never accept cash, vouchers or gifts during an open tender or contract negotiation.

## Confidentiality

Your employment agreement includes a confidentiality clause that continues after you leave. Confidential information includes customer data, source code, pricing, salaries, investor information and unreleased features. When in doubt, treat it as confidential.

## Intellectual property

Code, designs, documents and inventions you create in the course of your work belong to Nimbus Labs. Open source contributions made as part of your job are allowed under the Open Source Policy on the engineering wiki.

# 9. Hiring, Onboarding and Probation

## Hiring

Every open role has a written job description and a hiring panel of at least three people, including one person from outside the team. Candidates do a short paid take-home task (paid Rs. 5,000) or a live pairing session, never both. Offers are approved by the department head and the People team.

Offers depend on a background check covering identity, education and the last two employers. Checks are done by an external agency and usually take 7 to 10 working days.

## Your first week

- Day 1: laptop handover, accounts set up, meet your buddy and your manager.
- Day 2: product walk-through with the Product team, security training starts.
- Days 3 to 5: team rituals, first small task, 30-60-90 day plan agreed with your manager.

## Probation

Every new employee has a **probation period of 6 months**. Your manager reviews you at 3 months and again before the 6-month mark. Probation can be extended once by up to 3 months, with written reasons. During probation the notice period is **15 days** on either side.

Leave during probation: you earn leave from your first day but can use earned leave only after completing 3 months. Sick leave can be used from day one.

## Buddy programme

Each new employee is paired with a buddy from another team for the first 90 days. Buddies get Rs. 3,000 for team lunches during that time.

You earn 2 days of earned leave for every month worked, 24 days a year. Apply for earned leave of more than 2 days at least **7 days in advance** so your team can plan. You get 8 days of sick leave a year. Tell your manager on the first morning you are unwell. For more than 2 days in a row, upload a doctor's note on Pulse when you are back. Unused sick leave lapses at the end of the year. If you run out of sick leave, further sick days come from earned leave.

## Casual leave

Casual leave is for short personal matters: up to 6 days a year, at most 2 days at a time. It cannot be combined with earned leave to make a longer break.

Following the Maternity Benefit Act, women employees get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children and 12 weeks from the third child. Up to 8 weeks can be taken before the expected delivery date. After maternity leave, you may work from home for up to 3 months, by agreement with your manager. Fathers and secondary parents get 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption.

## Leave without pay

When all paid leave is used, you may ask for leave without pay for up to 30 days a year. It needs approval from your manager and the People team, and salary is cut for each day taken.

## Public holidays and optional holidays

## Leave encashment

Earned leave is not encashed during employment. On exit, up to 30 days of unused earned leave are paid out at your basic salary per day (monthly basic divided by 30). Sick and casual leave are never encashed.

## 12. Performance Reviews and Promotions

### Review cycles

Performance reviews happen twice a year: the **mid-year review in April** and the **annual review in October**. Pay revisions and promotions take effect from **1 November**, after the annual review.

### How a review works

- You write a self-review on Pulse covering goals, impact and growth areas.
- Two to four peers you choose, agreed with your manager, give written feedback.
- Your manager writes the review and proposes a rating.
- Ratings are calibrated across the department so that the same rating means the same thing in every team.
- Your manager shares the final review with you in a one-to-one meeting.

### Rating scale

|  Rating | Meaning | Typical pay revision  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  5 - Exceptional | Far above expectations; impact beyond the team | 12% to 18%  |
|  4 - Strong | Consistently above expectations | 8% to 12%  |
|  3 - Solid | Meets expectations for the level | 5% to 8%  |
|  2 - Developing | Meets some expectations; clear gaps | 0% to 3%  |
|  1 - Below | Does not meet expectations | 0%, performance plan  |

### Promotions

Promotions follow the career ladder on the People wiki. A promotion needs a written case from your manager, showing that you have worked at the next level for at least 6 months, and approval from the promotion committee. Promotions come with a pay increase of at least 10%.

### Performance improvement plan

An employee rated 1 is put on a performance improvement plan (PIP) of 60 days with clear, written goals and weekly check-ins. If the goals are met, the PIP closes. If not,

the company may end employment with the standard notice period.

# 15. Employee Stock Options (ESOP)

Most employees at Nimbus Labs are offered stock options, so that everyone shares in the company's success. The details of your grant are in your grant letter; this chapter explains how the plan works in general.

## Who gets options

Full-time employees at level 2 and above receive an options grant with their offer. Further grants (refreshers) may be given after the annual review to people rated 4 or 5, and on promotion.

## Vesting

Options vest over **4 years with a 1-year cliff**. This means 25% of your options vest on the first anniversary of your grant date, and the rest vest in equal parts every quarter over the next 3 years. If you leave before the first anniversary, no options vest.

|  Time since grant | Vested (cumulative)  |
| --- | --- |
|  Less than 12 months | 0%  |
|  12 months | 25%  |
|  24 months | 50%  |
|  36 months | 75%  |
|  48 months | 100%  |

## Exercising options

To own shares, you exercise your vested options by paying the exercise price written in your grant letter. While you work here, you can exercise at any time after vesting. If you leave, you have **5 years** from your last working day to exercise vested options. Unvested options lapse on your last working day.

## Tax

Exercising options creates a taxable benefit: the difference between the fair market value on the exercise date and the exercise price is taxed as salary. Selling the shares later may create capital gains tax. Talk to a tax adviser before you exercise.

## Buybacks

The company has run two buybacks, in 2024 and 2025, letting employees sell up to 20% of their vested shares. Future buybacks are not guaranteed.

## 19. Separation and Exit

### Notice period

After probation, the notice period is **60 days** for everyone, on either side. During probation it is 15 days. The company may accept a shorter notice period, or pay salary in place of notice. Earned leave cannot be used to shorten the notice period.

### Resigning

Submit your resignation on Pulse. Your manager and HR Business Partner will meet you within 3 working days. Your last working day is 60 days after the resignation date unless agreed otherwise.

### Exit checklist

- Hand over your work, documented on the team wiki.
- Return your laptop and accessories to IT **by your last working day**. Remote employees get a courier pickup.
- Clear any pending expense claims and return company cards.
- Complete the exit interview with the People team.

### Full and final settlement

The full and final settlement, including leave encashment and any pending reimbursements, is paid within **45 days** of your last working day. Your relieving letter and experience letter are emailed on the day of the settlement.

### Disciplinary process

For breaches of policy, the company follows a fair process: a written notice of the issue, a chance to respond within 5 working days, a decision by a panel that does not include your manager, and a right to appeal to the Chief People Officer within 7 days of the decision.

# 21. Payroll and Salary Structure

## Pay day

Salaries are paid on the **last working day of each month** into your bank account. Payslips are on Pulse on the same day. If you join after the 20th, your first month's salary is paid with the next month's salary.

## Salary structure

|  Component | Share of fixed pay | Notes  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Basic salary | 50% | Basis for PF, gratuity and leave encashment  |
|  House rent allowance (HRA) | 20% | Tax-free in part if you pay rent  |
|  Special allowance | Balance | Fully taxable  |
|  Employer PF contribution | 12% of basic | Part of cost to company  |
|  Work-from-home stipend | Fixed amount | See Hybrid work  |

## Tax declarations

Declare your planned tax-saving investments on Pulse by **30 April**. Submit proofs by **15 January**. Without proofs, tax is deducted as if no investments were made for the last three months of the financial year.

## Variable pay

Employees at level 4 and above have variable pay of 10% of fixed pay, paid once a year in May, based on company results and the annual rating.

## Salary advance

In an emergency, you may ask Finance for a salary advance of up to one month's net pay, repaid over a maximum of 6 months, interest free.

## 22. Expense Reimbursement

We pay back reasonable costs you spend on company work. Spend company money as if it were your own.

### How to claim

- Submit claims on Pulse under Expenses, with a clear photo or PDF of each receipt.
- Submit claims within **30 days** of spending. Claims older than 90 days are not paid.
- Claims submitted by the **5th of a month** are paid with that month's salary.
- Your manager approves claims up to Rs. 25,000; larger claims also need the department head.

### Expense limits

|  Policy code | Category | Limit  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  FIN-EXP-01 | Local travel (cab/auto) for work | Actuals, Rs. 3,000 per month  |
|  FIN-EXP-02 | Late night cab (office exit after 21:00) | Actuals  |
|  FIN-EXP-03 | Mobile and internet (non-WFH roles) | Rs. 1,000 per month  |
|  FIN-EXP-04 | Team lunch (once a quarter) | Rs. 1,200 per person  |
|  FIN-EXP-05 | Fitness membership | Rs. 1,500 per month  |
|  FIN-EXP-06 | Books and courses | From the learning budget  |
|  FIN-EXP-07 | Client entertainment | Rs. 3,000 per person, pre-approval needed  |
|  FIN-EXP-08 | Home office setup (one time) | Rs. 15,000  |

### Not reimbursed

- Alcohol, except client entertainment under FIN-EXP-07.
- Traffic fines and parking fines.
- Personal travel added to a business trip.
- Lost personal items, and upgrades to business class or premium economy.

### Company credit cards

Managers and people who travel often may get a company credit card. Limits and rules are in the Corporate Cards and Petty Cash chapter.

## 23. Travel Policy

### Booking

Book all work travel through the travel desk on Pulse at least 7 days before a domestic trip and 21 days before an international trip. Bookings made later need department head approval. Travel needs approval from your manager before booking.

### Class of travel

- Flights: economy class for all employees, on all domestic and international flights under 8 hours.
- Flights over 8 hours: premium economy for level 5 and above.
- Trains: AC 2-tier, or AC chair car for day trips.
- Local travel at the destination: cabs at actuals (the monthly FIN-EXP-01 limit does not apply on trips).

### Hotels and daily allowance

|  City tier | Examples | Hotel per night | Daily allowance  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  Tier 1 | Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru | Rs. 7,000 | Rs. 1,500  |
|  Tier 2 | Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata | Rs. 5,500 | Rs. 1,200  |
|  Tier 3 | All other Indian cities | Rs. 4,000 | Rs. 1,000  |
|  International | Any country | USD 180 | USD 60  |

The daily allowance covers meals and small costs, and is paid without receipts. If meals are provided (for example at a conference), the allowance is halved for that day.

### Travel insurance

International trips are covered by the company's travel insurance. Download the certificate from the travel desk before you fly.

## 24. Corporate Cards and Petty Cash

### Who gets a corporate card

Corporate credit cards are issued to managers, the Sales team, and employees who travel for work more than 4 times a year. Request a card on Pulse; Finance issues it within 10 working days.

### Card limits

|  Card holder | Monthly limit | Single transaction limit  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Employees who travel often | Rs. 50,000 | Rs. 25,000  |
|  Managers | Rs. 1,00,000 | Rs. 50,000  |
|  Department heads | Rs. 3,00,000 | Rs. 1,50,000  |

### Using the card

- Use the card only for company expenses that follow the Expense and Travel policies.
- Upload the receipt for every transaction on Pulse within 7 days.
- Personal spending on the card, even if repaid, is a breach of policy.
- Report a lost card to the bank and to finance@nimbuslabs.example immediately.

### Petty cash

Each office keeps a petty cash float of Rs. 20,000, held by the office manager, for small urgent purchases such as courier charges or repairs. Single petty cash payments are limited to Rs. 2,000. The float is reconciled with receipts every Friday.

# 25. Budgets and Cost Centres

## Financial year

Our financial year runs from **1 April to 31 March**, the same as the Indian tax year. Budgets are planned in February and approved by the board in March.

## Cost centres

Every employee and every expense belongs to a cost centre. Department heads own their cost centre's budget and review spending every month with Finance.

|  Cost centre | Code | Owner  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Engineering | CC-100 | VP Engineering  |
|  Product and Design | CC-200 | Head of Product  |
|  Sales and Marketing | CC-300 | Chief Revenue Officer  |
|  Customer Support | CC-400 | Head of Support  |
|  People and Admin | CC-500 | Chief People Officer  |
|  Finance and Legal | CC-600 | CFO  |

## Overspending

If a cost centre expects to spend more than 5% above its quarterly budget, the owner must tell the CFO in writing before the spend. Unplanned spending above 10% needs CEO approval.

## Month-end close

Finance closes the books by the **7th working day** of every month. Approve any pending invoices and expense claims in your queue before the 3rd working day, so they land in the right month.

## 26. Procurement and Approvals

This chapter covers buying software, services and equipment for the company.

### Approval matrix

|  Purchase value (per year) | Approval needed | Quotes needed  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Up to Rs. 50,000 | Manager | 1  |
|  Rs. 50,001 to Rs. 5,00,000 | Department head + Finance | 2  |
|  Rs. 5,00,001 to Rs. 25,00,000 | CFO | 3  |
|  Above Rs. 25,00,000 | CEO and CFO | 3 + legal review  |

### Software and SaaS

Any new tool that stores company or customer data needs a security review by IT before purchase, whatever its price. Free trials are fine, but never upload customer data to a tool under trial.

### Vendor onboarding

New vendors are added by Finance after receiving their GST certificate, PAN, cancelled cheque and signed contract. Vendors are paid on net 30 terms, in the payment run every Friday.

### Purchase orders

Purchases above Rs. 50,000 need a purchase order (PO) raised on Pulse before the vendor starts work. Invoices without a PO number are sent back.

# 27. Customer Billing and Collections

This chapter is for the Sales, Customer Success and Finance teams, but everyone should know how we charge customers.

## Plans and billing

|  Plan | Price | Billing  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Starter | Rs. 799 per month | Monthly, by card or UPI autopay  |
|  Growth | Rs. 1,999 per month | Monthly or yearly (2 months free)  |
|  Enterprise | Custom | Yearly invoice, net 30  |

## Discounts

Sales can offer up to 15% off a yearly Growth plan without approval. Discounts from 15% to 30% need the Chief Revenue Officer. Anything above 30%, or any free months beyond the standard offer, needs the CFO.

## Collections

Enterprise invoices are due 30 days after the invoice date. Finance sends reminders on day 31 and day 45. At 60 days overdue, the account is moved to read-only mode after the account manager has called the customer.

## Refunds

Monthly plans are not refunded for a partly used month. Yearly plans cancelled within 30 days of purchase are refunded in full; after that, unused months are refunded only with CFO approval.

## GST

All prices are shown before GST. GST at 18% is added to every invoice. Customers can add their GSTIN on the billing page to claim input tax credit.

# 28. Company Assets and Equipment

## Laptops

Engineers and designers get a MacBook Pro; other roles get a MacBook Air. Laptops are refreshed every **3 years**. You are responsible for the laptop's safe keeping. A laptop lost through negligence (for example, left in a parked car) may be charged to you at its depreciated value.

## Accessories

You may order a monitor, keyboard, mouse and headset from the IT catalogue on day one. Home office furniture is covered by FIN-EXP-08.

## Returning equipment

All equipment is returned on or before your last working day. Unreturned equipment is deducted from the full and final settlement at its depreciated value.

## Buying your laptop

When your laptop is refreshed, or when you leave after at least 2 years, you may buy your old laptop at its depreciated book value, after IT wipes it.

# 30. Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I work fully from home?

Only if you live more than 60 km from both offices and your department head and HR approve. Otherwise you work from the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.

## Who do I contact for payslip errors?

Raise a ticket on Pulse under Payroll, or email payroll@nimbuslabs.example. Errors raised before the 20th are fixed in the same month.

## Can I take earned leave during my notice period?

Only with your manager's approval, and it does not shorten the notice period.

## What happens to my insurance when I leave?

Cover ends on your last working day. You can convert it to an individual policy with the insurer within 30 days, at your own cost.

## How do I change my bank account?

Update it on Pulse before the 15th of the month, with a cancelled cheque. Changes after the 15th apply from the next month.

## Where can I find this handbook?

The latest version is always on the People wiki. Printed copies are out of date the day they are printed.
